## Week 5: January 29 - February 1

### New Skills: Runecrafting, Fletching, Crafting

*Commits: January 29 - February 1, 2026*

Three major production skills added with OSRS-accurate mechanics:

- **Runecrafting**: Craft elemental and combat runes at runecrafting altars
  - Rune types: Air, Mind, Water, Earth, Fire, Chaos
  - Multi-rune mechanics (craft multiple runes per essence at higher levels)
  - Essence types: Rune essence, Pure essence
  - XP scaling: 5.0-8.5 XP per essence
  - Manifest: `recipes/runecrafting.json`

- **Fletching**: Create bows and arrows from logs
  - Arrow shafts (15-90 per log depending on tier)
  - Headless arrows (shafts + feathers)
  - Unstrung bows (shortbows and longbows)
  - Bow stringing (bowstring + unstrung bow)
  - Arrow tipping (arrowtips + headless arrows)
  - Tiers: Normal, Oak, Willow, Maple, Yew, Magic
  - Manifest: `recipes/fletching.json`

- **Crafting**: Leather armor, dragonhide, jewelry, and gem cutting
  - Leather armor: Gloves, boots, cowl, vambraces, body, chaps, coif
  - Studded armor: Body and chaps (leather + steel studs)
  - Dragonhide: Green d'hide vambraces, chaps, body
  - Jewelry: Gold rings, amulets (accuracy, strength, power, glory, fury)
  - Gem cutting: Sapphire, emerald, ruby, diamond, dragonstone, onyx
  - Manifest: `recipes/crafting.json`
