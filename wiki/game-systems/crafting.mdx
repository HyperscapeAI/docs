The crafting skill allows players to create leather armor, dragonhide equipment, and jewelry. Using materials like leather, dragon leather, and gold bars, players can craft equipment for ranged combat and valuable accessories.

## Overview

Crafting is an artisan skill that produces:
- **Leather armor** for early ranged defense
- **Studded leather** for mid-level ranged combat
- **Dragonhide armor** for high-level ranged defense
- **Jewelry** including rings and amulets
- **Cut gems** for jewelry crafting

All crafting follows OSRS-accurate mechanics with proper level requirements, XP rewards, and tick timing.

## Training Methods

### Early Levels (1-40)
1. Craft leather gloves and boots (level 1-7)
2. Make leather bodies and chaps (level 14-18)
3. Create coifs for better XP (level 38)

### Mid Levels (40-60)
1. Craft studded leather armor (level 41-44)
2. Cut gems for profit and XP (level 20+)
3. Make jewelry at furnaces (level 50+)

### High Levels (60+)
1. Craft green dragonhide armor (level 57-63)
2. Create high-level jewelry (level 70-90)
3. Cut dragonstones and onyx (level 55-67)

## Stations

### Furnace
Used for smelting gold bars and crafting jewelry. Found in major cities and crafting areas.

**Jewelry Crafting:**
1. Have gold bar and mould in inventory
2. Use gold bar on furnace
3. Select jewelry type from interface
4. Wait for crafting to complete

### Tanning

Leather and dragon leather must be obtained by tanning hides at a tanner NPC. This is a prerequisite for most crafting activities.

## Related Systems

- **Smithing** — Create steel studs and gold bars
- **Mining** — Gather gold ore and gems
- **Ranged Combat** — Use crafted armor
- **Trading** — Sell jewelry and armor

## Equipment Bonuses

### Leather Armor
- Low defense bonuses
- Positive ranged attack bonus
- No negative magic penalties
- Suitable for early ranged training

### Studded Leather
- Moderate defense bonuses
- Enhanced ranged attack bonus
- Requires level 20 ranged and defence
- Good mid-level ranged armor

### Dragonhide Armor
- High defense bonuses
- Strong ranged attack bonus
- Good magic defense
- Negative melee attack penalties
- Best-in-slot for ranged combat

### Jewelry
- Various combat bonuses
- Prayer bonuses on higher tiers
- Strength bonuses for melee
- Accuracy bonuses for all combat styles

## Implementation Details

Crafting recipes are defined in `manifests/recipes/crafting.json` with:
- Input items and quantities
- Required tools (needle, chisel, moulds)
- Consumables (thread with 5 uses)
- Level requirements
- XP rewards
- Tick timing (OSRS-accurate)
- Station requirements (furnace for jewelry)

The system supports both portable crafting (leather, gems) and station-based crafting (jewelry at furnaces).
